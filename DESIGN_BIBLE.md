## 1. Extreme Tone Guide

### The voice
- **LOUD, FAST, AFFECTIONATE.** Like a Monster Jam announcer who genuinely loves everybody in the arena and has had three energy drinks.
- Short punchy lines. Exclamation is the default punctuation of the arena, not of every single sentence — save the caps for names, trucks, events, and huge moments so they still hit.
- Every NPC is a hype person for *something*: a truck, a trick, a shop part, a drink, the player.
- Failure is never shameful, it's a **WIPEOUT** — and wipeouts get replayed, laughed about, and immediately followed by "RUN IT BACK."
- Winning is never quiet. Winning is confetti, horns, Cherry Bomb losing her mind on the mic.

### The aesthetic
- Mountain Dew / Red Bull / Monster Energy energy — neon green, crimson red, black-and-acid — expressed **only through parody brands**:
  - **VOLTAGE DEW** — citrus blast, neon green
  - **CRIMSON BULL** — gives you horns, crimson red
  - **BEAST ENERGY** — unleash the snack, black + acid green
- **Never claim sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation with any real brand. Ever.** Parody names only, in every file, string, and asset.
- Visual shorthand: checker flags, chrome, mud, stadium lights, jumbotron type, sticker-slap logos, air horns rendered as text: `BWAAAAAP`.

### Writing rules
1. Replace, don't decorate: it's not a "village," it's RADHAVEN. Not a "potion," a can of VOLTAGE DEW.
2. Numbers go big: crowds are 60,000, jumps are 40 feet, trucks weigh 12,000 lb — match the real Monster Jam scale (see §7).
3. Every quest giver wants something EXTREME done, and says so in slang.
4. Tutorial text still has to teach. Extreme voice, clear instructions — bussin' AND readable.
5. Keep one honest line where it matters: prices, controls, and save warnings stay plain enough to act on.

## 2. Slang Dictionary

Use everywhere. Rotate so no single phrase gets stale.

| Slang | Means | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| hella | very / a lot | "That jump was hella clean." |
| straight bussin' | excellent, elite, top-tier | "Vixen's new shocks? Straight bussin'." |
| full send | commit 100%, no hesitation | "Don't lift. FULL SEND." |
| no cap | for real, no lie | "Mama Max has never lost a freestyle. No cap." |
| yeet | throw / launch with force | "Yeet that truck over the bus stack." |
| slap / slaps | hits hard, is great | "This track slaps." |
| rad | excellent (Mountain Dew rad) | "RADHAVEN on race day is so rad." |
| cook / let's cook | get to work, start the thing | "New parts installed. Let's fucking cook." |
| run it back | do it again immediately | "Wipeout? RUN IT BACK." |
| beefy | big, powerful, upgraded | "Level 5 top speed is BEEFY." |
| gnarly | intense, sketchy, impressive | "Gnarly two-wheel save, Roxy." |
| stoked | excited | "Whole pit crew is stoked." |
| wipeout | a crash — celebrated, not shamed | "Hall-of-fame wipeout." |
| send it | see full send, shorter | "Ramp's clear. SEND IT." |
| drip | style / livery / outfit game | "Sparkle Smash'd has elite drip." |

## 3. Character Sheets

### THE HERO

#### Garth "Mega Ultra Super Huge Dick" Elfstrom
- **Role:** Player character. Dungeon crawler turned monster truck driver. The new guy RADHAVEN is already printing shirts for.
- **Truck:** GARTH'S MEGA MONSTER (player truck, fully upgradeable at Vixen's Speed Shop)
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's fucking cook!"
- **Design notes:** Elf hero bones from Elfstory3d, remixed extreme — mullet energy, sleeveless denim over quest gear, VOLTAGE DEW green highlights, drives everything like he stole it (he didn't — Vixen has the receipts).

### THE CAST — all adult characters, all with real jobs, all exaggerated bombshell designs per Garth's brief (HUGE proportions, arena-poster energy)

#### Roxy Rampage
- **Role:** Freestyle queen. Teaches / judges Freestyle, owns the arena's big-air ramp.
- **Truck:** SPARKLE SMASH'D (co-featured; see roster) — glitter livery, zero fear.
- **Catchphrase:** "Backflip or it didn't happen, baby!"
- **Design notes:** Exaggerated bombshell silhouette, star-spangled firesuit half-zipped over a tank, huge teased hair that somehow survives a helmet, checker-flag nails. Every idle animation is a pose.

#### Vixen Voltage
- **Role:** Pit crew chief + owner of **Vixen's Speed Shop** — the between-races upgrade shop. The economy runs through her.
- **Truck:** Shop truck / tow rig (not raced) — chrome everything.
- **Catchphrase:** "Cash talks, nitro SCREAMS. What're we buying?"
- **Design notes:** Exaggerated bombshell silhouette, mechanic jumpsuit tied at the waist, wrench always in hand, grease smudge as makeup, BEAST ENERGY black + acid green. She never upsells you wrong — her prices are the prices in §6, straight up, no cap.

#### Cherry Bomb
- **Role:** Arena announcer. Calls Racing, 2-Wheel Skills, and Freestyle live; her lines are the game's hype track.
- **Truck:** None — she IS the jumbotron. Booth above the arena.
- **Catchphrase:** "RADHAVEN, ARE YOU REEEADY?!"
- **Design notes:** Exaggerated bombshell silhouette, crimson CRIMSON BULL red blazer, headset mic, voice that can be heard over 12,000 lb of V8. If a moment matters, Cherry narrates it.

#### Dixie Dewdrop
- **Role:** Pit Party host. Runs the pre-show: meet the drivers, see the trucks, mini-games, photo ops, drink stands.
- **Truck:** ZOMBIE PARTY BUS — she drives the fans in, obviously.
- **Catchphrase:** "Pit Party's OPEN, y'all — come touch a truck!"
- **Design notes:** Exaggerated bombshell silhouette, VOLTAGE DEW neon-green sundress-meets-racesuit, cowboy boots, clipboard of chaos. She knows every fan's name by the second show.

#### Mama Max
- **Role:** Reigning champion driver. The final boss of the bracket and the freestyle score to beat. Respectful rival, never a villain.
- **Truck:** BONE CRUSHER DELUXE — black, chrome skull stack, champion gold trim.
- **Catchphrase:** "Beat my score, sugar. I DARE you."
- **Design notes:** Exaggerated bombshell silhouette, champion belt over her firesuit, silver-streak hair, calm while everyone else screams. Losing to her feels like an honour; beating her ends Phase 3.

## 5. World Retheme Map

Elfstory3d → ElfStory Gone Wild. Invent in this spirit for anything not listed: if it's calm, make it a stadium; if it's dark, add stadium lights.

| Village pond (SE green) | **THE MUD PIT** | Trucks wash off here. Ducks remain. Ducks are extreme now. |

| Drink (parody) | Real-world energy | Effect | Where found |

## 6. Race / Shop Economy Tables

### 6.1 The original — Super Off Road arcade shop (reference)

Modelled on **Ivan "Ironman" Stewart's Super Off Road** (Leland arcade 1989, SNES 1992). Original arcade shop prices, per arcade-museum.com:

| Upgrade | Arcade price | Cap | Effect (original) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nitro | $1,000 each | Max 99 | Consumable boost |
| Tires | $40,000 | Max 5 levels | Tightens turning |
| Shocks | $60,000 | Max 5 levels | Less bounce |
| Acceleration | $80,000 | Max 5 levels | Faster acceleration |
| Top Speed | $100,000 | Max 5 levels | Higher top speed |

Source: arcade-museum.com entry for Super Off Road (cited Nov 15, 2026). These are the *original game's* numbers, kept here as the design reference — not our prices.

### 6.2 Our economy — scaled down, same structure

Same 5 categories, same caps (nitro stackable consumable; everything else max 5 levels), prices scaled down so one good race funds one meaningful upgrade:

| Upgrade | Our price (per level / unit) | Cap | Effect (ours) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nitro | $100 each | Max 99 | Tap-to-boost in race; pickup refills on track too |
| Tires | $400 / level | 5 | Tighter turning radius per level |
| Shocks | $600 / level | 5 | Less bounce, lands jumps cleaner |
| Acceleration | $800 / level | 5 | Faster off the line + out of corners |
| Top Speed | $1,000 / level | 5 | Higher max velocity |

### 6.3 Money in / money out

| Source | Pays |
|---|---|
| Race — 1st place | $1,000 prize |
| Race — 2nd place | $600 prize |
| Race — 3rd place | $350 prize |
| Race — 4th place | $150 prize (Cherry Bomb calls it a "character-building purse") |
| Cash bag pickup (on track) | $50 each |
| Crush a car (overworld) | $25 each |
| Quest / Dare complete | Varies, set per dare |

Design intent: 4th place + cash bags still buys nitro and saves toward Tires. 1st place buys a Top Speed level outright. The loop should always make the *next* race feel winnable — that's the Super Off Road magic, and we're keeping it, no cap.

## 7. Monster Jam Event Structure

Researched Nov 15, 2026 from Monster Jam show format: a real show is **Pit Party + head-to-head bracket Racing + 2-Wheel Skills + Freestyle**, with **12,000-lb trucks**, **fan judging** in freestyle, and iconic trucks including Grave Digger, El Toro Loco, Max-D, Megalodon, Sparkle Smash, and Zombie (our roster in §4 is original, inspired by — never copying — those icons).

Our full show (Phase 3 gate — runs in this order, in one session):

```
1. PIT PARTY        Dixie Dewdrop hosts. Meet drivers, touch trucks,
                    mini-games, drink stands, merch. No scoring, all vibes.
        │
2. RACING           Head-to-head bracket, single elimination.
                    Cherry Bomb on the mic. Winner gets bracket points.
        │
3. 2-WHEEL SKILLS   Wheelies, stoppies, bicycles. Best of 2 attempts,
                    fan judging. Roxy sets the score to beat.
        │
4. FREESTYLE        2 minutes: big air, backflips, donuts, wheelies,
                    car crushing. Fan judging decides it.
                    Mama Max closes the show as champion.
        │
5. CHAMPION         Highest total across events takes the night.
                    Beat Mama Max's total = dethrone the champion.
```

Scoring flavour: fan judging means the crowd meter matters — big air + variety + a hella wipeout recovered well can outscore a clean boring run. Straight bussin' beats safe, every time. That's not a bug in the judging; that's Monster Jam.
