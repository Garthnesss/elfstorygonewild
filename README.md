# ELFSTORY GONE WILD: EXTREME EDITION

**Hella extreme full remix of [Elfstory3d](https://github.com/Garthnesss/Elfstory3d). Straight bussin', no cap.**

You are **Garth "Mega Ultra Super Huge Dick" Elfstrom**. Explore RADHAVEN, talk to Roxy Rampage, Vixen Voltage, Cherry Bomb, Dixie Dewdrop and Mama Max, hop in your monster truck **GARTH'S MEGA MONSTER**, crush cars for cash, then full-send it into the arena for **SUPER OFF-ROAD REMIX** — a top-down, single-screen, 4-truck / 4-lap racer modelled on Ivan "Ironman" Stewart's *Super Off Road* (1989). Win cash, hit **VIXEN'S SPEED SHOP** between races, buy Nitro / Tires / Shocks / Acceleration / Top Speed, and go again faster.

Powered by (parody) VOLTAGE DEW, CRIMSON BULL and BEAST ENERGY. Not sponsored. Just rad.

## Play the v1 prototype

Open `docs/index.html` in a browser (also set up for GitHub Pages from `main` /docs). Single file, no build, no dependencies. Desktop WASD/arrows + E/T/Space, phone touch controls included.

- **RADHAVEN** — walk/drive, crush cars (+$50), collect drink cans (+nitro)
- **SUPER OFF-ROAD REMIX** — 4 trucks, 4 laps, nitro, mud, ramps, cash bags
- **VIXEN'S SPEED SHOP** — Nitro $100 · Tires $400/lv · Shocks $600/lv · Accel $800/lv · Top Speed $1000/lv (max 5)
